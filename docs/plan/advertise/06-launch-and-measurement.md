## 5. Interpretation

Use the snapshots for directional learning. GitHub traffic is a rolling window, external channels overlap, and repository activity has many confounders. Do not call a before/after change causal proof. Prefer questions such as “did qualified discovery broaden?” and “where did users get stuck?” over a single vanity metric.
